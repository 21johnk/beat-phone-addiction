FAQ

What is the Morning Makeover?

A simple three-step reset: get an alarm clock that isn't your phone, move your phone out of your bedroom, and check in with me. It takes about two days to get started, and I'm with you after that.

Why a separate alarm clock?

For most of us the phone is the alarm, so it sleeps next to the bed and is the first thing in our hands. A plain alarm clock removes that excuse, and lets the phone stay out of your bedroom.

What happens after I send the message?

I'll message you in two days to see how setup went. Reply YES once your alarm clock is set and your phone is out of the room. From there we talk about what comes next.
